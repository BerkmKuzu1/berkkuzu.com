About me

I am a fourth-year Electrical and Electronics Engineering student at Ostim Technical University, continuing my studies as the top-ranking student in my department with a GPA of 3.84 / 4.00. I work on digital design, embedded systems, artificial intelligence, and chip architecture and design.

Alongside my degree I work at Anadologic as an embedded systems and digital design engineer, currently on SpaceWire design to the ECSS-E-ST-50-12C standard. Before that I built Embedded Linux based Edge AI applications on Zynq UltraScale+ SoC FPGA platforms — hardware-accelerated computer vision on the DPU, and GStreamer pipelines carrying high-resolution industrial camera streams for real-time defect detection on production lines.

With the Ostim BLogic Microelectronics team, of which I am the founding captain, we are designing our own MCU architecture for the TEKNOFEST Chip Design competition. We passed the ÖTR preliminary design report stage and are a 2026 finalist team. My own role there is RTL design and FPGA prototyping on a Genesys 2 (Kintex-7).

I am well suited to teamwork and always open to learning — which is really what the log on this site is for. I frequently hear from those around me that I am organised, responsible and disciplined, and my goal is to create lasting value in high-tech projects.
