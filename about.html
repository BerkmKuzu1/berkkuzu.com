About Me

As a 3rd-year Electrical and Electronics Engineering student at Ostim Technical University (3.81 GPA), I have a deep passion for digital design, embedded systems, and chip architectures. Through my long-term internships during my undergraduate studies, I have taken on hands-on responsibilities across a wide spectrum—ranging from Edge AI development on Zynq SoC platforms to power electronics and hardware testing.

Currently, as the founding captain of the Ostim BLogic Microelectronics team, we are designing our own MCU architecture for the Teknofest Chip Design Competition. This role allows me to put my vision and dedication in this field into practice. As a highly motivated engineering candidate who is always open to learning and enjoys pushing the boundaries of hardware and software, my ultimate goal is to create lasting value in high-tech projects.

I am a strong team player and a lifelong learner. I am frequently recognized by my peers and colleagues for being organized, responsible, and highly disciplined.
